## Why illustrations, not photographs

Licensed sneaker photography was not available in the build environment, and downloading photos of real brands would raise licensing and trademark problems. Inventing a photograph would be dishonest. The illustrations are labelled as such in the site footer and on the About page.

## Replacing them with real photography

1. Obtain images you have the right to use (your own photographs, or licensed stock with the licence recorded here).
2. Export web-sized images with a consistent ratio (the cards and product page use a 1:1 crop) and save them under `frontend/static/images/products/` using the product slug as the filename.
3. Update the `image` path in `backend/seed.py` (or the database row) and add the source and licence to the table above.
4. Remove the "illustrations" notes from the footer and About page only when they are no longer true.

## No hotlinking

Every image and font is served from this repository. Nothing is loaded from a third-party URL, which also keeps the Content-Security-Policy strict (`img-src 'self'`).
