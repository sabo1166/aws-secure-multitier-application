Demonstration policies

Orders and payment

Orders placed here are demonstration records with the status “demo pending”. No payment is requested or processed and no goods are shipped. A real store would need a payment provider, fulfilment and a returns process.

Data we store

A demo order stores the name, email and shipping address you type, together with the items ordered. The shopping bag is held in a signed cookie in your browser. The newsletter form validates an address but does not store it. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.
