About

The Atelier

SOLE ATELIER is an independent sneaker boutique concept: limited releases, exceptional design, unmistakable presence.

We believe the best sneakers are chosen, not collected. Each silhouette on our shelf earns its place through proportion, restraint and a single considered detail. The result is a short list with a clear point of view.

What this site is

This is a portfolio and learning project: a working storefront built with Python (FastAPI), server-rendered templates and a relational database, designed to run on AWS. The products, prices, stock levels and journal entries are fictional demonstration content, and the product images are original illustrations rather than photographs.

Checkout records a demonstration order only. No payment is processed, no card data is collected and nothing is shipped. Nothing here is a claim that any product is authentic, limited or affiliated with any other brand.
